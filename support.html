中文常见问题

1. 免费版和 Pinvello Pro 一次性买断有什么区别？

  • 免费核心版：桌面最多同时保留 3 张便笺；全部 10 种界面语言、霞鹜文楷与全部本机系统字体、随壁纸自适应 / 经典暖阳主题、撕下飘落 / 柔和淡出 / 无动画与拟真音效免费可用；偏好设置内可免费预览全部 8 种删除动画与 6 款内置字体字形。
  • Pinvello Pro（终身买断，无订阅）：解锁无限便笺数量、全部 6 款内置开源手绘/设计字体、全部 6 套主题、手撕/打孔/横线/方格纸张、进阶固定件、全部 8 种删除动画保存、自定义动画时长（150–3000 ms）、一键自动排列与 doodles/、fonts/、themes/ 扩展包。

2. 更换设备或重装系统后如何恢复 Pro 买断权限？

请确保 Windows 中的 Microsoft Store 已登录购买时的同一 Microsoft 账号，打开 Pinvello 偏好设置 顶部横幅，点击「恢复购买」即可自动同步授权。

3. 便笺数据存放在哪里？如何备份或迁移？

全部本地数据保存在 %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pinvello\notes.json。直接备份该文件及同目录下的 extensions\ 即可完整保留所有便笺内容与配置。

4. 如何快速调节便笺倾角与清理桌面排版？

  • 鼠标悬停或单击进入编辑便笺时，按 Ctrl + Alt + ← / → 按 0.5° 步进调节倾角，Ctrl + Alt + 0 水平归零。
  • 按 Win + D 显示桌面后，便笺依然留在桌面图标下方、壁纸之上。

English Support & FAQ

1. What is included in Free vs. Pinvello Pro?

  • Free Tier: Up to 3 active desktop sticky notes at once, all 10 UI languages, LXGW WenKai plus all installed Windows system fonts, Adaptive & Classic themes, Peel Drop / Fade Out / None animations with procedural Foley audio, and unrestricted live previews of all Pro animations & curated fonts inside Preferences.
  • Pinvello Pro (One-Time Lifetime Purchase): Unlimited notes, all 6 curated SIL OFL fonts, all 6 themes, Torn/Perforated/Ruled/Grid paper styles, extended fasteners (Striped Tape, Pearl Pin, Paperclip, Binder Clip, Wall Hook), all 8 delete effects, custom durations (150–3000 ms), Auto-Arrange Notes, and external doodles/, fonts/, themes/ extensions.

2. How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new PC or after reinstalling Windows?

Sign in to the Microsoft Store app with the Microsoft account you used to purchase Pinvello Pro, then open Pinvello Preferences and click Restore Purchase.

3. Where are my sticky notes stored?

All notes and local preferences are saved offline inside %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pinvello\notes.json. Back up that file together with extensions\ anytime.

4. Need further assistance?

Please open a ticket on our public support tracker: https://github.com/botmiao/pinvello-docs/issues.
